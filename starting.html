Starting Your Experience

Establish professional communication, run your first meetings and finalize your partnership agreements.

Phase 2 of 5

  2. Start (you are here)
  3. During
  4. End

Why this phase matters

First impressions shape the course of a partnership. Clear team contracts, shared agendas and early agreement on scope build trust with your client and psychological safety within your team.

Step by step

Template

15 min

Send your first email

Keep it short, warm and specific, and make it easy to say yes to a meeting.

Include:

  • Who you are and who's on your team
  • What you understand about their goals
  • Two or three meeting times

Guide

60 min

Run a kickoff meeting

Take notes and send a recap within 24 hours.

Sample agenda:

  1. Introductions (10 min)
  2. The partner's goals and what success looks like (20 min)
  3. Constraints and timeline (10 min)
  4. Check-ins and next steps (20 min)

Template

30 min

Write a team contract

A short agreement everyone signs is easier to point back to than to argue about later.

Decide together:

  • How fast you reply to messages
  • How you make decisions when you disagree
  • What happens if someone misses a deadline
