Preparing for Your Experience

Set the foundation, understand your community's context and check your team's readiness before the work begins.

Phase 1 of 5

  1. Prepare (you are here)
  2. Start
  3. During
  4. End
  5. After

Why this phase matters

Entering a community or client setting without examining your own assumptions can lead to misaligned expectations or unintended harm. Preparing thoughtfully lets you approach partners as a collaborative learner, not an outside “expert” fixing a problem.

Step by step

Worksheet

20 min

Set your learning goals

Aim for two or three goals specific to this experience.

Finish these sentences:

  1. By the end, I want to be able to…
  2. One skill I want to practice is…
  3. Something I want to learn about this community is…

Guide

30 min

Examine your assumptions

Looking honestly at your own identity and privilege helps you enter as a learner.

Ask yourself:

  • What do I assume about this organization or place?
  • How might my background shape how I see this work?
  • Who could help me understand the context?

Checklist

15 min

Understand what's expected

Find your course, partner and team expectations in writing.

Before day one:

  • List every deliverable and due date
  • Find out the expected hours per week
  • Ask about NDAs, data or photo agreements
  • Write down who to contact if something goes wrong
