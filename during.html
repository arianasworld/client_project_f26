During Your Experience

Practice adaptive project management, human-centered fieldwork and constructive feedback as your project unfolds.

Phase 3 of 5

  1. Prepare
  2. Start
  3. During (you are here)
  4. End
  5. After

Why this phase matters

Real projects rarely follow a straight line: requirements shift, teams change and research turns up surprises. Learning to manage scope creep and act on feedback is where real growth happens, so treat friction as part of the process, not a barrier.

Step by step

Worksheet

30 min

Observe with AEIOU

Sort what you see in the field so you can analyze it later.

Record:

  • Activities people are doing
  • Environments they're in
  • Interactions between people and systems
  • Objects they use
  • Users who are there

Guide

10 min a week

Keep your sponsor updated

Short, regular updates build trust and prevent surprises.

Each update covers:

  1. Progress: what's done
  2. Next: what's coming and when
  3. Asks: what you need from them

Guide

20 min

Work through conflict

Disagreement is normal. Address it early and respectfully.

Try this:

  1. Raise it privately, focusing on the work
  2. Listen before you respond
  3. Agree on one change and check back in a week
  4. Still stuck? Ask your instructor or the ELO
